How the price works

The price comes from 10 years of both records at that exact spot, for the same two weeks of the year, so the split rule is priced in. The quote server signs it and the contract checks the signature.

Settling buys two Telegraph jobs (1 USDC each, paid from the pool): one from an Open-Meteo miner, one from a NASA POWER miner. Both reach your limit: 100%. They split: 50%. Neither: 0. No person decides.

A replay costs the payout plus 2 USDC, so it can never make money. It exists so you can watch a whole settlement today.
