Cover a day against rain

Pick a place, a day and how much rain would ruin it. If both independent sources record at least that much, the contract pays you. Sepolia testnet, test USDC only.

1 · Wallet
You need a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby…) with a little Sepolia ETH for gas.
No Sepolia ETH? Use a free faucet, e.g. Google's.
2 · What to cover
Real cover: for a day 1–90 days ahead. Settles about 6 days after the day, because NASA publishes late.
3 · Price
4 · Your policy

The price comes from 10 years of rain history at that spot. The underwriter signs it and the contract checks the signature. Settling buys two Telegraph jobs (1 USDC each, from the pool): one from an Open-Meteo miner, one from a NASA POWER miner. Both reach your limit: 100% paid. They split: 50%. Neither: 0. No person decides. The cover price uses 10 years of both sources at that spot, so the split rule is priced in. A replay costs the payout plus 2 USDC, so it can never make money. It exists so you can watch a whole settlement today.
